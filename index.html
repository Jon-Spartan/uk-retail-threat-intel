        Retail watch

        Editorial notes on security stories that matter to UK retail.

        Shopping scams · 24 November 2026

        Give shoppers a way back to the real store

        The NCSC reviewed its online shopping guidance just before Black Friday. It advises shoppers to find a retailer’s official site themselves and use passkeys or two-step verification where available.

        For retailers Make order tracking and scam reporting easy to find on your own site.

        NCSC shopping guidance

        Supplier incidents · 23 November 2026

        When a supplier reports a breach

        The Register reported a breach at an online supplier to Next. The retailer said it had found no evidence that customer personal data was affected at the time of its statement. Its investigation was still open.

        For retailers Agree who confirms the impact and who answers customers when a supplier reports an incident.

        The Register’s reporting

        Payment data · 20 November 2026

        Payment data after checkout

        Cleverbridge began notifying affected customers after finding that data had been copied from its platform. The provider says some of the copied data included payment card details.

        For retailers Ask payment providers which card data they retain and how they notify affected customers.

        Disclosure checked · 25 November The provider’s FAQ says card security codes were involved in a minority of cases. Affected customers should check their own notification for details.

        Cleverbridge incident update

        Cyber watch

        Major cyber stories from the UK and around the world.

        US financial reporting · 25 November 2026

        FinCEN extends some filing deadlines after disruption

        The US Treasury’s financial crimes bureau took some systems offline while it investigated unauthorised access. FinCEN gave eligible filers extra time to submit certain reports.

        Takeaway Know where to check for deadline changes if a regulator’s filing portal goes down.

        The Register FinCEN order (PDF)

        European public services · 23 November 2026

        EU Academy goes offline after a data leak

        The European Commission confirmed that a wider cyber incident exposed personal data from its learning platform. It suspended public access while it assessed how many users were affected.

        Takeaway Plan how to reach users through a separate channel when the service they normally sign in to is unavailable.

        Update · 25 November The Commission said the investigation was still open and gave no new assessment of its scope. Commission follow-up

        European Commission briefing

        UK transport · 19 November 2026

        Oyster cards kept working as TfL investigated a breach

        Transport for London told The Register it took the Oyster photocard website offline after an incident affecting customer accounts. Existing photocards could still be used for travel.

        Takeaway In an incident update, spell out which services customers can still use and where to get help.

        The Register’s TfL reporting

        Curated edition · 19–25 November 2026

        • Check internet-facing services

          The NCSC reported an increase in AI-assisted attacks and urged defenders to revisit basic controls such as account permissions and access to public-facing services.

          NCSC guidance, 24 November

        • Plan for customer notifications

          Cleverbridge’s update explains what it has confirmed about payment data. The ICO’s breach guide helps teams work through what to assess, record and report when personal data is involved.

          Cleverbridge disclosure · ICO breach guide

        • Keep an alternative route open

          Disruption at FinCEN affected some filings, while people could still travel on their existing Oyster photocards during the portal outage. Both show the value of explaining what still works.

          FinCEN story · Oyster story
