# Sources and attribution

## Automated intelligence

- **CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog** supplies catalogue entries and the confirmed exploitation signal. CISA releases the catalogue under CC0 1.0. Catalogue inclusion confirms an exploitation report at the vulnerability level. Check the vendor advisory for product-specific applicability. [CISA catalogue](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [CISA's catalogue licence](https://github.com/cisagov/kev-data/blob/develop/LICENSE)
- **CVE Program** supplies CVE identifiers and available structured CVSS base scores. The dashboard keeps them separate from its own priority scoring. The CVE List is copyrighted by The MITRE Corporation. The release does not include copies of complete CVE Records. [CVE List](https://github.com/CVEProject/cvelistV5) · [CVE licence and copyright notice](https://www.cve.org/Legal/TermsOfUse)
- **EPSS** scores are generated by Empirical Security and published by the EPSS Special Interest Group at FIRST. The dashboard displays the score alongside the relevant CVE and observation date when available. [FIRST EPSS](https://www.first.org/epss/) · [Attribution guidance](https://www.first.org/epss/faq)

## Reviewed UK guidance

These reference notes are separate from the live feed and describe the source guidance in project-authored text.

- **National Cyber Security Centre, NCSC Annual Review 2026, Embedding cyber security into everything, 3 November 2026.** The chapter emphasises practical baseline controls, including keeping software current and managing access to systems. Contains Crown copyright information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. [NCSC review](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/annual-review-2026/building-strong-foundations/embedding-into-everything) · [NCSC reuse terms](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/about-this-website/terms-and-conditions) · [Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)

The ICO marks this guidance as under review. [ICO guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/security/a-guide-to-data-security/) · [ICO reuse terms](https://ico.org.uk/global/copyright-and-re-use-of-materials/) · [Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)

## Release policy

The release starts with an empty checked-in snapshot. Public datasets and briefing evidence are limited to CISA catalogue records. The collection workflow validates candidate data before artifact upload and validates it again when restoring data or staging the site. The reviewed UK guidance above is published as a static reference.

Review collection permission, redistribution terms and attribution before enabling an additional feed. For UK public-sector items, check individual rights notices and exclude any third-party material outside the licence. Keep publisher headlines and excerpts out of the public branch and artifacts unless permission covers their intended use.
